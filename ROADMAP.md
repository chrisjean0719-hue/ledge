# Roadmap

Ledge plans one timber raised bed over HTTP, can add a liner, can add a drainage layer, can place a stake at each corner, counts its sleepers and soil, returns the elevation as an SVG, and compares two beds. The six updates are complete. About 30 hours remain.

## After the sixth update

These stay after the six updates. They are about 30 hours.

### Cap rail (3 hours)

Add a sitting rail on the top course and count that timber separately.

### Soil mix (2 hours)

Split the soil into compost and topsoil by a chosen mix.

### Mesh floor (2 hours)

Add a mesh under the drainage and report its area.

### Dividers (3 hours)

Split the bed into two or three plots and count the divider timber.

### Path edge (3 hours)

Add a short path strip along one long side and report its length.

### Water volume (3 hours)

Estimate the water held in the soil from a chosen saturation.

### Cut list (3 hours)

List each sleeper length once for each course, with the corners marked.

### Plant count (3 hours)

Place plants on a grid inside the bed and report how many fit.

### Print sheet (3 hours)

Lay the counts and the elevation out for one printed page.

### Trellis back (3 hours)

Add a trellis on one long side and count its uprights and bars.

### Hardware (2 hours)

List the screws, the stakes, and the liner clips for the plan.

A cap rail is the next piece. The API can compare two beds, and it does not yet add a sitting rail.
